# Runbook 2026-11-08: Dograh 1.51.0, caller memory, record lookup, call outcomes

The owner runs this in a terminal (not the `!` box: several steps are interactive). Nothing here was run by the agent that wrote it;
every step marked **[unverified offline]** depends on the live Railway project, Dograh or Synchronize and could not be checked
without them. Secrets are typed only at silent reads (`IFS= read -rs`), never as arguments, and are unset at the end. What changed
and why: `UPGRADE-1.51.md` and `workflows/README.md` (Caller memory, Live wiring, Evals). A line that says `STOP` means stop there;
a line that says `go to R` means stop and roll back from the "Where to enter" table in R.

Order that matters:

- Steps 1–4 (Dograh 1.51.0) do not depend on Synchronize and can run first. Run steps 2–3 when no call is expected: if Railway keeps
  the 1.49 api serving while the new one migrates, an English call in that minute ends without its spoken goodbye (`UPGRADE-1.51.md`,
  Behaviour callers notice; not verifiable offline).
- Step 5 (go-live) needs the Synchronize release with the caller-work route and the pre-call caller fields (branch
  `fm/261108_voice_caller_context`) deployed and healthy: the probe refuses to continue without `/api/voice/tools/caller-work`. It posts the
  run id as a JSON integer, as the lookup tool's body template does, but Synchronize answers a body it refuses like an unknown run, so only
  test call 2 (step 8) shows the integer is accepted.
- Step 5 needs `$FORK` clean at the reviewed commit or a later one that contains it (`golive.py` reads the templates from `HEAD`).
- Step 6 (evals) needs only step 5. Step 7 routes the Korean number only after step 6's results are read, so the first real call reaches
  the wired workflow and nobody hears the new Korean prompts before they were measured.
- Step 8 ends with a measurement block (response time, silence, names, voices). Its results decide what to tune next; they do not roll
  anything back by themselves.

   today's date once for the row under way, no price, no promise, nothing about the open Task or its due date, and the owner will follow
   up on anything else. The run shows a `lookup_caller_work_ko` tool call with HTTP 200. Compare what you heard with the record in Synchronize: Call QA cannot (its transcript
   shows that the lookup ran, not what it returned), so this call is the only check that a relayed record is faithful. Hearing that the record
   cannot be checked now although the test data is there means the lookup failed or Synchronize did not accept the run id: the run shows
   the tool call's status, and the Synchronize log shows `Dograh caller-work body refused` for a refused id.
   **Unconfirmed variant.** Call once more from the same phone, say `다음 방문 언제 오세요?`, and answer the name question with
   `아니요, 가족인데 이름은 괜찮아요`. Hear no `확인해 보겠습니다.` (the lookup is only for a caller who confirmed the name) and nothing from the

   from a caller who did not confirm the name (`workflows/README.md`, Caller memory); this is its only live check.
3. **Outcome label.** After hanging up, wait about a minute (the outcome is classified after the hangup). Synchronize's 전화 tab shows the call's
   label from the six outcomes (call 2, a question about an existing visit, should read as `message`), and the Dograh run's `call_disposition` is that code.

   A call still showing `user_hangup` after five minutes means the post-call classifier failed: read the dograh-api log.
4. **English line.** Call `$TO_NUMBER_EN`, give a reason and a name, and follow the call to the goodbye. Hear the greeting with the business
   name; after about a minute the 전화 tab shows the call with a label from the six outcomes. This call decides whether R1 rolls back English.
5. **Silence after the greeting.** Call `$TO_NUMBER_KO` and say nothing. About 3 s after the greeting hear one question whether you are
   still there, and if you stay silent a short goodbye and the hang-up a few seconds later (`UPGRADE-1.51.md`, Silence after the greeting).

   user message (`api/services/workflow/pipecat_engine_callbacks.py:31-45`), which the post-call extraction may read as the caller's words.
6. **Barge-in.** Call, say `견적 문의 좀 하려고요`, and while the agent is still saying its next sentence talk over it with three or more words
   (`아니요 잠깐만요 다시 말할게요`). Hear it stop within about a second and answer what you said, not finish its sentence. The Korean line
   needs three words to interrupt (`turn_start_min_words: 3`); also try the two-word `잠깐만요 잠깐만요` once and write down whether it stops.
7. **"사장님 바꿔 주세요".** Call and say `사장님 바꿔 주세요`. Hear, with `죄송하지만`, that no one can take the call right now, and one question

   and finish. After the call, the Dograh run's `follow_up` (gathered context) carries the time, and Synchronize shows it on the call.
8. **Leak report.** Call and say `욕실 천장에서 물이 새요`, give a name and one detail. Hear the message taken and no arrival time promised.
   After about a minute the 전화 tab shows the call with the 긴급 badge and the outcome `booking_request` or `message`.
